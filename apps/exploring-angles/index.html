Exploring Angles

Definition 8
Ordinary Angle
An angle is an ordinary angle if its arms are not on one line, and its inside is a convex set.

Angles — local learning note
— AngleAngle is a primitive term in geometry. An angle has a vertex, two arms and an inside (interior).
Angle as a subset of the planeThe inside of an angle is a subset of the plane: it is a set of points in the plane.
ConvexFor any two points in the set, the whole segment joining them stays in the set.
Non-convexThere are two points in the set for which part of the segment joining them lies outside the set.
Default meaningUnless otherwise stated, angle means ordinary angle.
Current angleAn acute angle is an ordinary angle.

Primitive term

A primitive term is a basic mathematical word that is accepted without being defined from earlier geometric terms.

We use primitive terms such as point, line, plane and angle to build later definitions.
