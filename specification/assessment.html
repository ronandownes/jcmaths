Assessment and Reporting

Assessment involves gathering, interpreting and using information about the processes and outcomes of learning. It can record and report achievement, determine appropriate routes through a differentiated curriculum, and identify areas of difficulty or strength.

The focus of assessment and reporting is on the improvement of student learning. The junior cycle places a strong emphasis on assessment as part of the learning process and on assessment methods being fit for purpose, timely and relevant to students.

Focused feedback is a critical component of high-quality assessment. Assessment is most effective when it moves beyond marks and grades and identifies the next steps for further learning.

Formative

Assessment close to the point of learning, used to support next steps.

Diagnostic

Used to identify particular strengths, gaps or areas of difficulty.

Summative

Records achievement as one element within a broader assessment approach.

Feedback

Focused feedback supports learners in managing their learning and persisting with complex tasks.

Classroom-Based Assessments

CBA 1 — Mathematical investigation

FormatPreparationCompletion
Report in a range of possible formatsThree-week mathematical investigation following the problem-solving cycleEnd of second year

Problem-solving cycle: define a problem; decompose it into manageable parts and/or simplify it using appropriate assumptions; translate it into mathematics if necessary; engage with and solve the problem if possible; interpret findings in the context of the original problem.

CBA 2 — Statistical investigation

FormatPreparationCompletion
Report in a range of possible formatsThree-week investigation following the statistical enquiry cycleEnd of first term of third year

Statistical enquiry cycle: formulate a question; plan and collect unbiased, representative data; organise and manage the data; explore and analyse it using appropriate displays and numerical summaries; answer the original question with reasons based on the analysis.

Assessment Task & Exam

Assessment Task and final examination

The Assessment Task is a written task completed during class time and sent to the State Examinations Commission for marking. It is related to the learning outcomes on which the second Classroom-Based Assessment is based.

Final examination

There is an Ordinary-level and a Higher-level examination paper, set and marked by the State Examinations Commission. The learning outcomes assessed in any year constitute a sample of the relevant outcomes.

Inclusive assessment practices

Reasonable accommodations may be put in place where a student has a specific physical or learning difficulty. These accommodations should be consistent with the supports the school has put in place for the student’s learning throughout the year.
