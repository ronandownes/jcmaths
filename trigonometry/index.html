Trigonometry sits within the combined Geometry and Trigonometry strand. Its learning outcome is the H2 used by this dropdown.

GT.4 — Trigonometric ratios

Evaluate and use the trigonometric ratios \(\sin\), \(\cos\) and \(\tan\), and their inverses, for right-angled triangles.

For a right-angled triangle, \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\), \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\), and \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}\).
