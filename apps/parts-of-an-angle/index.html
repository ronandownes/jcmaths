Naming an angle

∠ABC   or   ∠CBA

The middle letter is the vertex. Here, B is the vertex.

In a simple case where there is only one angle at B, it may also be named ∠B.

Unless otherwise specified, you may take it that any angle we talk about is an ordinary angle.

What to notice

To each angle is associated:

1. a unique point B, called its vertex;

2. two rays [BA and [BC, both starting at the vertex, and called the arms of the angle;

3. a piece of the plane called the inside of the angle.

Check the language

What do we call each ray that starts at the vertex?
An arm of the angle.
What do we call the common endpoint of the two arms?
The vertex. The plural is vertices.

Arm of an angle

Definition 1 gives the ray language: a point divides a line into two pieces, called rays.

The ray that starts at B and passes through A is written [BA; similarly, [BC starts at B and passes through C.

For an angle, these two rays are called the arms of the angle.
