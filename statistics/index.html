Statistics

Statistics sits within the combined Statistics and Probability strand. SP.3 is the H2 learning outcome that feeds this dropdown.

SP.3 — Statistical investigation

Carry out a statistical investigation: generate a question; plan and collect unbiased, representative data; classify data; select and interpret displays; calculate and interpret summary statistics; evaluate displays; discuss misuse of statistics; and discuss assumptions and limitations of conclusions.

A statistical investigation moves from a clear question to appropriate data, representation, analysis and a conclusion that acknowledges limitations.
