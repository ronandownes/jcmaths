Junior Cycle Mathematics

Unifying Strand

For now, this page uses the Unifying Strand learning outcomes from the Junior Cycle Mathematics specification as its structure. Each learning outcome is an H2 so it appears directly in the Unifying dropdown.

U.1 — Fundamental concepts and procedures

Recall and demonstrate understanding of the fundamental concepts and procedures that underpin each strand.

U.2 — Apply procedures

Apply the procedures associated with each strand accurately, effectively and appropriately.

U.3 — Equality

Recognise that equality is a relationship in which two mathematical expressions have the same value.

U.4 — Represent mathematics in different ways

Represent a mathematical situation in a variety of different ways, including numerically, algebraically, graphically, physically and in words; and interpret, analyse and compare such representations.

U.5 — Connections within mathematics

Make connections within and between strands.

U.6 — Mathematics and the real world

Make connections between mathematics and the real world.

U.7 — Make sense of problems

Make sense of a given problem and, if necessary, mathematise a situation.

U.8 — Solve problems

Apply knowledge and skills to solve a problem, including decomposing it into manageable parts and/or simplifying it using appropriate assumptions.

U.9 — Interpret solutions

Interpret a solution to a problem in terms of the original question.

U.10 — Evaluate solutions

Evaluate different possible solutions to a problem, including reasonableness, improvements and limitations.

U.11 — Generalise and conjecture

Generate general mathematical statements or conjectures based on specific instances.

U.12 — Arguments and proofs

Generate and evaluate mathematical arguments and proofs.

U.13 — Communicate mathematics

Communicate mathematics effectively: justify reasoning, interpret results, explain conclusions, and use mathematical language and notation precisely.
