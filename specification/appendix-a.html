Appendix A: Glossary of action verbs

This glossary clarifies the learning outcomes. Each verb is described in terms of what the learner should be able to do once the learning outcome has been achieved.

Analyse
Study or examine something in detail; identify essential elements, parts and relationships, and interpret information to reach conclusions.
Apply
Select and use knowledge and/or skills to solve a problem in a new situation.
Calculate
Work out a numerical answer.
Classify
Group things based on common characteristics.
Compare
Give an account of similarities and/or differences between items or situations, referring to each throughout.
Construct
Use properties of shapes and geometric results to draw accurately using prescribed geometrical tools.
Convert
Change from one form to another.
Define
Give a rule that identifies the elements of a set.
Discuss
Offer a considered, balanced review that includes a range of arguments, factors or hypotheses, supported by appropriate evidence.
Estimate
State or calculate a rough value for a quantity.
Evaluate
Judge the relative quality or validity of something.
Explain
Give a reasoned account showing how causes lead to outcomes.
Generalise
Generate a general statement based on specific instances.
Generate
Produce or create.
Interpret
Use knowledge and understanding to explain the meaning of something in context.
Investigate
Observe, study or make a detailed and systematic examination to establish facts and reach new conclusions.
Justify
Give valid reasons or evidence to support an answer or conclusion.
Mathematise
Generate a mathematical representation, such as a graph, equation or geometric figure, to describe an aspect of a phenomenon.
Prove
Give a deductive argument demonstrating that a statement is true, including reasons for each step.
Round
Give the closest number in the required form.
Sketch
Draw a rough diagram or graph without geometrical tools.
Solve
Work out an answer or solution.
State
Provide a concise statement with little or no supporting argument.
Understand
Have detailed knowledge of, use appropriately, and see connections between parts.
Use
Apply knowledge or rules to put theory into practice.
Verify
Demonstrate that a statement is true.
