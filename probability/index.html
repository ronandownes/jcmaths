Probability

Probability sits within the combined Statistics and Probability strand. SP.1 and SP.2 are the H2 learning outcomes that feed this dropdown.

SP.1 — Outcomes and sample spaces

Investigate outcomes of experiments, generate sample spaces systematically, and use the fundamental principle of counting.

For two coin tosses, a sample space is \(\{HH,HT,TH,TT\}\).

SP.2 — Random events and probability

Investigate random events, understand probability on a 0–1 scale, calculate probability for equally likely outcomes, and use relative frequency as an estimate of probability.

For equally likely outcomes, \(P(E)=\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{number of possible outcomes}}\).
