Axiom 1

Two Points Axiom

Start with two distinct points, A and B. Drag either point and watch the unique line through them change.

There is one line through A and B. Move either point to test it.

Axiom 1 (Two Points Axiom)
There is exactly one line through any two given points.

We denote the line through A and B by AB.
