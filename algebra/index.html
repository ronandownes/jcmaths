Junior Cycle Mathematics

Algebra and Functions

The learning outcomes below follow the wording of the Junior Cycle Mathematics specification. Each learning outcome is an H2 and therefore feeds this strand’s dropdown automatically.

AF.1 — investigate patterns and relationships (linear, quadratic, doubling and tripling) in number, spatial patterns and real-world phenomena involving change so that they can:

  1. represent these patterns and relationships in tables and graphs
  2. generate a generalised expression for linear (and quadratic) patterns in words and algebraic expressions and fluently convert between each representation
  3. categorise patterns as linear, non-linear, quadratic, and exponential (doubling and tripling) using their defining characteristics as they appear in the different representations

AF.2 — investigate situations in which letters stand for quantities that are variable so that they can:

  1. generate and interpret expressions in which letters stand for numbers
  2. find the value of expressions given the value of the variables
  3. use the concept of equality to generate and interpret equations

AF.3 — investigate situations in which letters stand for quantities that are variable so that they can:

  1. add, subtract and simplify
    i. linear expressions in one or more variables with coefficients in \(\mathbb Q\)
    ii. quadratic expressions in one variable with coefficients in \(\mathbb Z\)
    iii. expressions of the form \(a/(bx+c)\), where \(a,b,c\in\mathbb Z\)
  2. multiply expressions of the form
    i. \(a(bx+cy+d)\); \(a(bx^2+cx+d)\); and \(ax(bx^2+cx+d)\), where \(a,b,c,d\in\mathbb Z\)
    ii. \((ax+b)(cx+d)\) and \((ax+b)(cx^2+dx+e)\), where \(a,b,c,d,e\in\mathbb Z\)
  3. divide quadratic and cubic expressions by linear expressions, where all coefficients are integers and there is no remainder
  4. flexibly convert between the factorised and expanded forms of algebraic expressions of the form:
    i. \(axy\), where \(a\in\mathbb Z\)
    ii. \(axy+byz\), where \(a,b\in\mathbb Z\)
    iii. \(sx-ty+tx-sy\), where \(s,t\in\mathbb Z\)
    iv. \(dx^2+bx\); \(x^2+bx+c\); (and \(ax^2+bx+c\)), where \(b,c,d\in\mathbb Z\) and \(a\in\mathbb N\)
    v. \(x^2-a^2\) (and \(a^2x^2-b^2y^2\)), where \(a,b\in\mathbb N\)

Interactive: Simplify Expressions

AF.4 — select and use suitable strategies (graphic, numeric, algebraic, trial and improvement, working backwards) for finding solutions to:

  1. linear equations in one variable with coefficients in \(\mathbb Q\) and solutions in \(\mathbb Z\) or in \(\mathbb Q\)
  2. quadratic equations in one variable with coefficients and solutions in \(\mathbb Z\) or coefficients in \(\mathbb Q\) and solutions in \(\mathbb R\)
  3. simultaneous linear equations in two variables with coefficients and solutions in \(\mathbb Z\) or in \(\mathbb Q\)
  4. linear inequalities in one variable of the form \(g(x)<k\), and graph the solution sets on the number line for \(x\in\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb R\)

AF.5 — generate quadratic equations given integer roots

AF.6 — apply the relationship between operations and an understanding of the order of operations including brackets and exponents to change the subject of a formula

AF.7 — investigate functions so that they can:

  1. demonstrate understanding of the concept of a function
  2. represent and interpret functions in different ways—graphically (for \(x\in\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb R\), [continuous functions only], as appropriate), diagrammatically, in words, and algebraically—using the language and notation of functions (domain, range, co-domain, \(f(x)=\), \(f:x\mapsto\), and \(y=\)) (drawing the graph of a function given its algebraic expression is limited to linear and quadratic functions at OL)
  3. use graphical methods to find and interpret approximate solutions of equations such as \(f(x)=g(x)\) and approximate solution sets of inequalities such as \(f(x)<g(x)\)
  4. make connections between the shape of a graph and the story of a phenomenon, including identifying and interpreting maximum and minimum points
